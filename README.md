## Pizza Sales Analysis - Power BI Project

### Overview

This Power BI project provides a comprehensive analysis of pizza sales data, offering key insights into revenue, order trends, and popular pizza types. It's designed to help pizza businesses optimize their offerings, understand peak demand, and improve overall sales performance.

### Key Metrics

*   **Total Revenue:** \$817.86K
*   **Average Order Value:** \$38.31
*   **Total Pizza Sold:** 49,574
*   **Total Orders:** 21,350
*   **Average Pizza Orders:** 2.32

### Key Insights

*   **Daily Trend:** Orders are highest on weekends, especially Friday and Saturday evenings.
*   **Monthly Trend:** Maximum orders are observed in July and January.
*   **Pizza Category Performance:** The Classic category contributes the most to total sales.
*   **Pizza Size Preference:** Large-size pizzas are the most popular.
*   **Best Sellers:** The Thai Chicken Pizza contributes the most to revenue, while the Classic Deluxe Pizza leads in total quantities sold and total orders.
*   **Worst Sellers:** The Brie Carre Pizza contributes the least to revenue, total quantities sold, and total orders.

### Visualizations

*   **Daily Trend for Total Orders:** Line chart showing order volume throughout the week.
*   **Monthly Trend:** Line chart visualizing order trends across different months.
*   **% of Sales by Pizza Category:** Pie chart displaying the distribution of sales across pizza categories (Classic, Supreme, Chicken, Veggie).
*   **% of Sales by Pizza Size:** Pie chart illustrating the proportion of sales for each pizza size (Large, Medium, Small, X-Large, XX-Large).
*   **Top 5 Pizza by Revenue, Quantity, and Total Orders** Bar charts showcasing the top-performing pizzas.
*   **Bottom 5 Pizza by Revenue, Quantity, and Total Orders:** Bar charts highlighting the underperforming pizzas.

### How to Use This Project

1.  Download the Power BI project file.
2.  Open the file in Power BI Desktop.
3.  Interact with the dashboards and reports to explore the data and gain insights into pizza sales performance.
